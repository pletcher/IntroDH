# Introduction to Digital Humanities – Midterm Exam

Using the guidelines published by DH at Berkeley <https://live-digital-humanities-berkeley.pantheon.berkeley.edu/resources/technical-evaluation-digital-humanities-projects>, you will evaluate one of the projects
from the 2025 DH awards: http://dhawards.org/dhawards2025/results/.
Working in groups of 3 or 4, submit a 2– to 3–page evaluation _by the end of class_.

This submission is not expected to be as polished as the submission that you prepared
for homework, but you have the entire class period to work on it.
