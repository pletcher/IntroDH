# Introduction to Digital Humanities – Final Exam

Working in groups of 3 or 4, design a tutorial for someone new to digital humanities.

What topics do you cover? What are the important readings? Are there similar projects
to which you could point?

Your tutorial must cover some aspect of programming with Python.

You will be evaluated based on your critical engagement with the materials covered
this semester. "Critical" means just that: feel free to suggest revisions to some
of the topics that we covered, or to discuss ways that you might approach a problem
differently.

This exam is both an opportunity for you to reflect on your learning and to propose
new ways of learning the material.

Your submission may take the form of a lesson plan, an outline, or a standard paper.
It is due at the end of the class period.


## Individual component

Separately from your group work, write a half-page to 1-page reflection
on your contributions to the tutorial. Specify which sections or components you personally
developed or helped to develop. You might also discuss what you might
have done differently.
